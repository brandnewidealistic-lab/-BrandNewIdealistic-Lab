# Automation Guide
Step‑by‑step instructions for automating content creation.

---

## 🧠 Overview
This guide walks you through how to build a hands‑free content creation system using **Microsoft Copilot**, **Canva**, and **CapCut**. The goal is to automate your daily workflow — from generating ideas to publishing finished content — so you can focus on creativity and strategy.

---

## ⚙️ Step‑by‑Step Instructions

### 1. Generate Daily Prompts with Copilot
- Open Microsoft Copilot and use it to create motivational or educational prompts.  
- Example: “Write a short motivational quote about resilience.”  
- Save each prompt in your `/prompts` folder for reuse.  
- Keep them short, emotionally charged, and easy to adapt for video or image content.

### 2. Edit Visuals in Canva or CapCut
- Import your saved prompts into **Canva** or **CapCut**.  
- Use faceless templates, background music, and consistent branding (colors, fonts, tone).  
- Export your finished visuals or videos to the `/assets` folder.  
- Recommended formats: `.mp4` for videos, `.png` or `.jpg` for images.

### 3. Schedule Posts Using Automation Tools
- Connect your `/assets` folder to **Make.com** or **Zapier**.  
- Create a scenario that automatically uploads new files to your social platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube).  
- Add scheduling logic — for example, post every morning at 8 AM.  
- Optional: track engagement metrics in **Notion** or **Airtable** for performance analysis.

---

## 🔄 Example Workflow
Each arrow represents a trigger or integration. Once configured, your system runs automatically — generating, editing, and posting content without manual effort.

---

## 🧩 Optimization Tips
- Keep prompts concise and emotionally impactful.  
- Use consistent branding across all visuals.  
- Schedule posts during peak engagement hours.  
- Review analytics weekly to refine your automation logic.

---

## 🏁 Goal
To create a **self‑sustaining AI content factory** that transforms ideas into published media with minimal human input — empowering you to scale your creative output effortlessly.
