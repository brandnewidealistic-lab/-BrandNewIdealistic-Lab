# Assets Folder
This folder stores all creative output generated by your AI‑powered content system. Every video, image, audio file, template export, and resource used in your workflows is organized here for easy access, automation, and scaling.

---

## 🎥 Video Assets
This section contains all exported videos created through CapCut, Canva, or other editing tools.

**Includes:**
- Daily motivational videos  
- Faceless content clips  
- Short‑form vertical videos (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)  
- Template‑based video exports  
- Background footage and B‑roll  

**Recommended formats:**  
- `.mp4` (primary)  
- `.mov` (optional)

---

## 🖼️ Image Assets
All static visuals used for thumbnails, posts, or templates.

**Includes:**
- Canva thumbnail exports  
- Quote images  
- Backgrounds and overlays  
- Branding elements (logos, color palettes, gradients)

**Recommended formats:**  
- `.png` (high quality)  
- `.jpg` (compressed)

---

## 🔊 Audio Assets
Any sound files used for video editing or automation.

**Includes:**
- Background music  
- Sound effects  
- Voiceover exports  
- AI‑generated narration files  

**Recommended formats:**  
- `.mp3`  
- `.wav`

---

## 🧩 Template Exports
Files generated from reusable templates.

**Includes:**
- Canva template exports  
- CapCut template exports  
- AI‑generated layout files  
- Visual frameworks for faceless content

---

## 📁 Automation‑Ready Structure
Your automation tools (Make.com, Zapier, etc.) will monitor these folders for new files.

Suggested structure:

**Purpose:**  
Automation triggers activate when new files appear in these subfolders, allowing hands‑free uploading and scheduling.

---

## 🧠 Best Practices
- Keep filenames consistent (e.g., `2026-09-18_daily_motivation.mp4`).  
- Store only final versions here — drafts go in `/workflows` or `/prompts`.  
- Clean out unused assets monthly to keep automation fast.  
- Use subfolders to keep Make.com/Zapier triggers organized.

---

## 🏁 Goal
To maintain a clean, structured, automation‑friendly library of all creative output — enabling your AI content factory to run smoothly, scale efficiently, and stay organized.
